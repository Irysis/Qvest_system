## Core Claim

**Paper A (Chue 2015)**: Mutual funds that move together with past winning strategies (co-winners) are crowded and underperform. Funds with idiosyncratic performance relative to the winner group (low GMP beta) have true skill and outperform co-winners by 0.342%/month.

**Paper B (Bruno, Chincarini & Ohara 2018)**: Portfolio crowding arises structurally from the MVO optimization process itself, independent of alpha signal similarity. Long-only portfolios are structurally more crowded (C ≈ 0.85–0.86) than market-neutral portfolios (C ≈ 0.00) due to the weights-sum-to-1 constraint. Applying Marchenko-Pastur RMT adjustment to the covariance matrix reduces crowding by 14% while maintaining portfolio quality.

## Crowding Detection Method (exact metrics if available)

**Paper A — GMP Beta:**
- GMP factor = Good-Minus-Poor = returns of past winning strategy minus past losing strategy
- For each fund, regress monthly returns on GMP factor to get GMP beta
- High GMP beta = fund moves with winners = co-crowded
- Co-winner funds underperform idiosyncratic winners by 0.342%/month (4.1%/yr) after Carhart 4-factor adjustment
- Data: CRSP mutual fund quarterly holdings + monthly returns

**Paper B — Cosine Similarity Crowding Measure (C):**
- For two portfolios i and j with weight vectors w_i, w_j:
  - Similarity: s_{ij} = (w_i' w_j) / (|w_i| |w_j|) [cosine similarity]
- For M portfolios, similarity matrix S = (H'H) ∘ H_hat
- **Crowding C** = average off-diagonal element of S = (Σ_{i≠j} s_{ij}) / (M² - M)
- Range: [0, 1] for long-only; [-1, 1] for long-short
- **Omega (Ω)** = C_portfolio / C_alpha = ratio of post-construction crowding to pre-construction crowding
  - Ω > 1: portfolio construction increased crowding beyond what alpha models implied

**MPA (Marchenko-Pastur Adjusted) Covariance:**
1. Decompose empirical covariance: Σ_r = U Λ U'
2. Identify significant eigenvalues (outside Marchenko-Pastur distribution upper bound: λ+ = (1 + sqrt(p))²)
3. Keep only significant K eigenvalues; reconstruct: Σ_r^MPA = U* Λ* U*'
4. Use Σ_r^MPA instead of Σ_r in MVO
- Result: C declines from 0.85 to 0.73 (14% reduction) for long-only; Ω ratio also declines

**Key findings on long-only structural crowding:**
- Crowding C = 0.85–0.86 for long-only with weights-sum-to-1 constraint
- This crowding is STATISTICALLY SIGNIFICANT at 99% confidence level even for random alpha signals
- Removing the sum-to-1 constraint causes C to collapse to ~0.03
- Sector constraints increase crowding further

## Key Results
- Long-only portfolios are intrinsically crowded; this is not due to similar alphas but due to optimization mechanics
- MPA reduces but does not eliminate structural crowding
- Crowding peaked just before 2007 quant crisis in all models (Figs 2, 3)
- Risk model homogeneity: if 90% of managers use one risk model, C = 0.85; equal distribution of 3 models gives C = 0.51 (40% reduction)
- Transaction costs (Paper P076, sequel): crowding DECLINES as portfolio size grows from $500M to $5B due to market impact forcing diversification into more stocks
