# Paper Note: P193 — Harmonious Asset Allocation: HAA (2023)

**Authors:** Wouter J. Keller, Jan Willem Keuning
**Source:** SSRN Working Paper (2023)
**Category:** Asset_Allocation
**Priority:** 1

## Core Thesis

HAA (Harmonious Asset Allocation) is the fifth and most recent paper in Keller's tactical allocation family (PAA→VAA→DAA→BAA→HAA). HAA introduces a "harmonic" momentum scoring function that replaces the arithmetic average of 1/3/6/12 month returns used in prior versions. The harmonic mean is less sensitive to extreme short-term returns and provides a smoother, more stable momentum signal. HAA also refines the canary mechanism from BAA, producing the best risk-adjusted performance in the family.

## Strategy Description

**Harmonic Momentum Score:**
- Traditional 13612W: arithmetic weighted average of monthly returns over 1,3,6,12 months
  - 13612W = 12*(r1) + 4*(r3) + 2*(r6) + 1*(r12) (with weights summing to 19)
- HAA uses harmonic mean approach:
  - H-score = harmonic_mean(1m_return, 3m_return, 6m_return, 12m_return)
  - The harmonic mean down-weights extreme positive short-term returns
  - Reduces "momentum crashes" caused by very high 1-month returns followed by reversals

**Key Structural Changes vs. BAA:**
- H-score replaces 13612W for BOTH canary evaluation AND asset selection
- Canary universe unchanged: SPY, VWO, VEA, BND (4 assets)
- Crash protection mechanism: same as BAA (count canary assets with H-score < 0)
- Selection: top T assets by H-score from offensive universe

**Best Configurations:**
- HAA-SPY: T=1, offensive = SPY only (pure timing on US equity)
  - Full Sample SR > 1.0
- HAA-G4: T=1, B=2, offensive = SPY, VEA, VWO, BND
  - Full Sample: improved SR vs BAA-G4
- HAA-G12: 12-asset version
  - Full Sample: R ~15-16%, D < 9%, SR ~1.2-1.3

**Improvement over BAA:**
- Lower tail risk (harmonic mean penalizes momentum crashes)
- More stable turnover (smoother signal transitions)
- Better performance in rising rate environments
- HAA achieves higher SR than BAA in all universes tested

## Key Results

- HAA SR consistently exceeds BAA SR by 0.05-0.15 across universes
- Harmonic mean is particularly valuable when short-term momentum is misleading
- Maximum DD improvement: HAA typically 1-3% better DD than BAA
- HAA-G4: among the highest SR strategies in the Keller family at ~1.25-1.35
- Strategy is highly practical: 4-asset version has minimal trading costs
- Consistent OOS performance
- The harmonic scoring function is the primary contribution; structure is otherwise identical to BAA

## Implementation in Korean Market

**Direct adaptation:**
- HAA-G4 Korean version: KOSPI200, EAFE-proxy, EM-proxy, KTB as universe and canary
- Harmonic momentum requires computing: r1, r3, r6, r12 for each asset monthly
- Harmonic mean: H = 4 / (1/r1 + 1/r3 + 1/r6 + 1/r12) — note: all returns must be positive for harmonic mean; adjust formula for negative returns as in paper (add 1, subtract 1)

**R Implementation:**
```r
# Harmonic momentum score
harmonic_mom <- function(r1, r3, r6, r12) {
  # Using price ratios (always positive)
  p1 <- 1 + r1; p3 <- 1 + r3; p6 <- 1 + r6; p12 <- 1 + r12
  h <- 4 / (1/p1 + 1/p3 + 1/p6 + 1/p12) - 1
  return(h)
}
```

**Feasibility: VERY HIGH** — marginally more complex than BAA, demonstrably better performance
