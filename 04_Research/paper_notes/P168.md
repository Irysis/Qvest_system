# P168: Smart Equity Investing — Risk Optimization on Strategic Beta Portfolios (2018)
**Category:** Smart_Beta | **Priority:** 3

## Core Finding
Extension of P167 adding risk optimization (min variance, max diversification, risk parity, equal weighting) to dependent vs. independent sorted portfolios. Dependent sort + risk optimization outperforms 42% of risk-oriented ETFs vs. only 31% for independent sort. Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage improves covariance estimation robustness. Transaction costs modeled via Hasbrouck (2009) Gibbs sampling show that risk optimization adds net value even after realistic costs. The combination of proper sorting methodology + risk optimization is stronger than either alone.

## Key Risk/Concern for Korean Implementation
Korean covariance matrices are notoriously unstable: the correlation structure shifts dramatically during crisis periods (2008, 2011 Euro crisis, 2020 COVID), making historical covariance estimates unreliable for min-variance optimization. Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage is essential but may need a shorter shrinkage window for Korea (24 months rather than 36–60 months) given faster correlation regime changes.

## Actionable Takeaway
1. In the portfolio optimization step, always use Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage with a 24-month estimation window for the Korean covariance matrix; implement this in R using the corpcor or nlshrink package already available in the environment.
2. Test dependent sort + risk parity weighting as an alternative to equal-weight within the ensemble_engine.R factor composite: weight each factor's contribution to portfolio risk equally rather than weighting each factor equally by score.
