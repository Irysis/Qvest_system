# P006: An Alternative Method to Construct Levered Indexes (2014)

## Core Finding
Daily-rebalanced leveraged ETFs (e.g., 2X, -1X S&P 500) systematically underperform their naïve benchmark (the static multiple of cumulative index return) due to a compounding/volatility drag effect. The return differential equals approximately -2 * r_t * r_{t+1} per period, meaning high-volatility, mean-reverting markets amplify the shortfall. A monthly-rebalanced alternative levered index construction substantially reduces this drag.

## Key Metrics
- Alpha/Return: 2X daily ETF returned -1.30% in 2011 vs. naive expectation of +4.22% (2 x 2.11%); expectation error of ~-5.52%
- Statistical strength: Empirical demonstration on S&P 500 data 2010-2011
- Sample: US S&P 500 ETF products, 2010-2011

## Korea Applicability
MEDIUM — Korean leveraged ETFs (KODEX 레버리지, KODEX 인버스) have the same mathematical structure. The volatility drag formula R_LETF ≈ β * R_underlying - (β²-β)/2 * variance applies directly. During high-volatility Korean market regimes, the shortfall can be substantial.

## Strategy Implication
When using Korean leveraged ETFs (KODEX 레버리지 2X) as portfolio overlay tools, account for volatility drag. In the regime detection framework: during high-VIX / high-realized-vol regimes, avoid levered ETF overlays. For the Maxwell Protocol, if leverage is used via ETF products, compute expected drag as (beta^2 - beta)/2 * annualized_variance and subtract from expected return in the hurdle gate calculation.
