## Core Thesis

Across 8 US inflationary episodes since 1926 and international evidence from 4 developed markets, trend following is the single best strategy for protecting real wealth during inflationary periods. Equities, bonds, and 60/40 portfolios all suffer significant real losses during inflation. Commodities (especially energy) provide positive real returns but are volatile. All-asset trend following achieves +25% average real return with 100% hit rate across inflationary regimes.

## Strategy Description

**Inflation Regime Definition:**
- Two conditions must BOTH hold:
  1. YoY CPI is accelerating (this month's CPI YoY > last month's CPI YoY)
  2. YoY CPI level >= 5%
- 8 US inflationary regimes identified from 1926-2020

**All-Asset Trend Following Construction:**
- Diversified across equities, bonds, commodities, currencies
- Signal: sign of trailing 12-month return for each asset
- Long if positive, short if negative (or exit to cash for long-only)
- Equal volatility weighting across assets

**Equity Cross-Sectional Momentum:**
- Rank stocks by trailing 12-1 month return
- Long top decile, short bottom decile
- Delivers +8% real return during inflation periods

## Key Results

- Trend following is the ONLY strategy with 100% hit rate across all 8 inflationary periods
- Passive inflation hedges (gold, TIPS, real estate) are inconsistent
- Equities hurt badly during inflation: -7% real return
- The inflation regime definition (accelerating + >= 5% CPI) identifies distinct market stress periods
- International evidence (UK, Germany, Japan) confirms: trend following beats all alternatives
- Commodity trend outperforms passive commodity exposure
- Active equity strategies (momentum, quality) modestly protective; passive equity is not

**Recent Relevance (2021 publication):**
- TIPS real yields had gone negative → expensive hedge
- Paper argues trend following is now the primary viable inflation hedge
- COVID-era commodity supercycle aligns with paper's analysis

## Implementation in Korean Market

- Korean CPI: also available via FRED (CPALTT01KRA657N)
- `macro_regime.parquet`: Extend to add inflation_regime indicator

**Korean inflation regime signal:**
```r
# Add inflation regime to macro_regime.parquet
inflation_regime <- macro_fred %>%
  filter(series == "CPIAUCSL") %>%  # US CPI
  mutate(
    cpi_yoy = (value / lag(value, 12) - 1) * 100,
    cpi_accel = cpi_yoy > lag(cpi_yoy, 1),
    inflation_regime = as.integer(cpi_accel & cpi_yoy >= 5)
  )
```
