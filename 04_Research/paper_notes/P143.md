# Paper Note: P143 — Fama & French "Dissecting Anomalies" (2008)

**Authors:** Eugene F. Fama, Kenneth R. French
**Journal:** Journal of Finance, Vol. 63, No. 4 (2008), pp. 1653-1678
**Category:** Factor_Return / Quality_Profitability_Factors
**Priority:** 2

---

## Core Thesis

Comprehensive cross-sectional study that "dissects" 6 prominent anomalies — net stock issuance, accruals, net operating assets, momentum, asset growth, and profitability — across size groups (microcap, small, big). Key findings:

1. Most anomalies are **microcap-specific** and disappear or weaken for big stocks
2. **Profitability (Y/B, earnings-to-book)** works for small stocks but is **weak for big stocks**
3. **Accruals and net operating assets** are robust across size groups
4. **Asset growth** is robust across size groups
5. **Momentum** is strong across all sizes

This paper laid the groundwork for FF5 by identifying investment and profitability as the key "missing" factors from FF3.

---

## Anomalies Studied

| Anomaly | Variable Definition |
|---------|-------------------|
| **NSI** (Net Stock Issuance) | log(shares_t / shares_{t-1}), split-adjusted |
| **Acc** (Accruals) | ΔWC - Dep / avg(AT) where WC = current assets - current liabilities |
| **NOA** (Net Operating Assets) | (AT - cash - STI - LT debt - deferred taxes - minority interest) / lagged AT |
| **Mom** (Momentum) | 12-2 month past return |
| **AG** (Asset Growth) | ΔAT / AT_{t-1} |
| **Y/B** (Profitability) | Income before extraordinary items / Book equity |

## Key Interpretations

### Why Profitability Fails for Big Stocks (in 2008 paper):
- FF (2008) uses Y/B = earnings / book equity — a different definition than later papers (which use gross profitability GP/A)
- Earnings-to-book is susceptible to earnings manipulation and accruals contamination
- **Later correction (FF 2015, P144):** RMW using *operating* profitability (OP) IS significant for big stocks — the 2008 finding was partly a measurement issue

### Microcap Dominance:
- Microcaps (NYSE bottom 20% by cap) constitute 60% of stocks but only 3% of total market cap
- Anomaly "strength" in equal-weighted returns dominated by microcap effects
- Value-weighted returns (which matter for institutional strategies) show much weaker effects
- **For Korean KOSPI200+KOSDAQ150 strategies: use value-weighted or cap-weighted sorts**

### Accruals as a Robust Signal:
- Accruals anomaly (-0.27% for big stocks, t=-3.66) is among the most consistent signals
- Different from other "quality" signals: accruals measure **earnings quality** not level
- Low accruals = cash earnings ≈ reported earnings → genuine quality signal

## Factor Interaction Analysis

### Controlling for Multiple Anomalies Simultaneously:
When all 6 anomalies are included in FM regressions:
- NSI, Acc, AG, Mom all remain significant
- NOA becomes insignificant (captured by Acc + AG)
- **Y/B becomes negative and insignificant for big stocks** (confirms the size-dependency finding)

### Implications for Multi-Factor Models:
- Accruals and asset growth jointly capture the "investment quality" dimension
- Momentum and profitability capture separate dimensions
- This motivated FF's development of RMW and CMA as new factors in FF5

## Dissection Framework Applied to Korea

**For Korean KOSPI200+KOSDAQ150 strategy:**

1. **Value-weight everything**: Korea's market cap is dominated by KOSPI200 large-caps. Equal-weighting would give false impression of signal strength. Use cap-weighted portfolio performance.

2. **Y/B (earnings-to-book) is NOT the right profitability measure**: Consistent with this paper's finding for big stocks. Use GP/A (Novy-Marx P139), CFOA (Bouchaud P141), or OP (FF2015 P144) instead.

3. **Accruals ARE robust for Korean large-caps**:
   ```
   Accruals = (ΔCA - ΔCash - ΔCL + ΔSTD + ΔTP - Dep) / avg(AT)
   ```
   DART provides all components; implement as quality gate (low accruals = good quality)

4. **Asset growth for Korea**: Significant in FF 2008 even for big stocks, but Wahal (P140) shows it disappears pre-1963. Use as secondary signal with haircut.

5. **NOA for Korea**: Net operating assets captures the full investment-minus-accruals signal — useful composite.
   ```
   NOA = (자산총계 - 현금및현금성자산 - 단기금융상품 - (자산총계 - 부채총계 -
          자본총계 - 이연법인세부채 - 소수주주지분)) / lagged 자산총계
   ```

## Connection to FF5 (P144)

This 2008 paper directly motivated FF5:
- Identified investment (AG, NOA) and profitability as "missing" factors
- Showed Y/B profitability is weak for big stocks → motivated switch to OP (operating profitability)
- AG / NOA patterns → motivated CMA (conservative minus aggressive)
- Together with Novy-Marx (2013), this paper is the empirical foundation for the five-factor model
