# P204: Equity Factor Investing: Historical Perspective of Recent Performance (2020)
**Category:** Risk_Portfolio | **Priority:** 3
**Authors:** Benoit Bellone, Thomas Heckel, Francois Soupe, Raul Leote de Carvalho (BNP Paribas Asset Management) | **Date:** October 23, 2020

## Core Thesis
The 2018-2020 multi-factor drawdown was NOT unprecedented and was primarily driven by **value factor underperformance** — not a general failure of factor investing. Quality factors (ROC, FCF/Assets, low accruals) and momentum factors remained positive throughout. The paper argues the underperformance was temporary and cyclical, comparable to the 1998-2000 tech bubble and 2008-2009 GFC. Key prescription: beta neutralization, macro-sector neutralization, constant target volatility, and equal risk contribution weighting are essential structural choices that substantially mitigated drawdowns.

## Application to Korean Factor Strategy

**The 2018-2021 factor crash — what happened and why:**
1. The primary driver was **growth stock outperformance** (tech mega-caps globally: FANMAG, TSMC, Samsung Electronics)
2. Value stocks (high B/M, high earnings yield) massively underperformed growth
3. COVID-2020 amplified: lockdown beneficiaries (tech, e-commerce, healthcare) vs. lockdown victims (financials, energy, traditional value)
4. In Korea specifically: Samsung Electronics and KAKAO dominated returns; traditional KOSPI value stocks (steel, chemicals, financials) deeply underperformed
5. Low-volatility stocks struggled because COVID risk was non-linear — low-vol stocks in services/tourism were hammered despite historically low measured volatility

**Is Korea similarly affected?**
Yes, but with structural amplifiers:
- Korea has heavier concentration in tech (Samsung ~30% of KOSPI200) than MSCI World in any single sector
- Korean value stocks heavily concentrated in financials + heavy industry — same sectors that underperformed globally
- Korea-specific: Chaebol holding company discounts mean "cheap" stocks often stay cheap due to governance discount
- **Recommendation**: In Korean market, apply sector-neutralization strictly (within GICS Level 2) to avoid tech vs. non-tech as pseudo-factor

**Prescriptions adopted for our pipeline:**
1. Beta neutralization: Already implemented in residual decomposition step — critical, confirmed
2. Sector neutralization: Add within-GICS-sector ranking to factor composite
3. Equal risk contribution: Implement ERC weighting in `ensemble_engine.R` across factor styles rather than equal weight
4. Constant target volatility: Implement ex-ante vol targeting (annual 10-15%) with monthly rebalancing
5. Drop raw size factor: Consistent with paper's finding that size added no value in mid-large cap universes
