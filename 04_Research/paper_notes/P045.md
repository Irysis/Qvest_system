# Paper Note: P045 — Shi & Zhang (2008-2010)
# "On Alternative Measures of Accruals"

**Authors:** Linna Shi (Syracuse), Huai Zhang (Nanyang Tech)
**Category:** Factor_Return / Accrual_Factors

## Core Thesis
Balance-sheet accruals (ACCR_BS) significantly outperform cash-flow statement accruals (TACCR_CF) as factor signals. The 4.2%p difference arises from non-articulation events and accrual component misclassification.

## Key New Finding
- **ACCR_BS: 8.3% annual return** vs **TACCR_CF: 4.1% annual return**
- Return gap (4.2%p) driven by: deferred taxes, equity in earnings, PP&E gains, other OCF items
- ~60% of firms have non-articulation issues (BS changes ≠ CF statement accruals)
- **Recommendation: USE ACCR_BS, not CFS method**, for stronger anomaly

## Korean Market Implication
⚠️ For STR_012: Use **balance-sheet method** (not CFS method) as primary accrual measure
- DART may have similar non-articulation prevalence in Korean IFRS filings
- Validate both measures on KOSPI200 sample before selecting

## Anomaly Status
**Robust and persistent** — measure-dependent. BS method stronger and more persistent.
