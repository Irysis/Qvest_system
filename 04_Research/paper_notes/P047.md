# Paper Note: P047 — Sloan Accruals (1996)

**Author:** Richard G. Sloan (U Penn)
**Journal:** The Accounting Review, Vol. 71, No. 3, July 1996, pp. 289-315
**Category:** Factor_Return / Accrual_Factors
**Priority:** 1

---

## Core Thesis

Investors fixate on reported earnings and fail to correctly weight the differential persistence of:
- **Accrual component** → lower persistence (mean-reverts)
- **Cash flow component** → higher persistence (real earnings quality)

Result: High-accrual firms are **overpriced** (negative future returns). Low-accrual firms are **underpriced** (positive future returns).

Hedge portfolio L/S earns **+10.4% size-adjusted abnormal return** in year t+1.

---

## Key Methodology

**Accrual (Balance Sheet Method — Sloan 1996):**
```
Accruals = (ΔCA - ΔCash) - (ΔCL - ΔSTD - ΔTP) - Dep
Accrual_Component = Accruals / Average_Total_Assets
```

**CFS Method (preferred for K-IFRS post-2011):**
```
Accrual_CFS = (Net_Income - CFO) / Average_Total_Assets
```
Sign: **Low accrual = good** (earnings backed by cash = LONG)

**Mishkin (1983) efficiency test:**
- Market prices accrual persistence at 0.911 (actual: 0.765) → over-weighted
- Market prices cash flow persistence at 0.826 (actual: 0.855) → under-weighted
- χ²= 180.91, p = 0.000 → strong efficiency rejection

## Implementation Blueprint (R Code)

```r
# CFS method (post-2011 K-IFRS)
dart_fund %>%
  arrange(ticker, fiscal_year) %>%
  group_by(ticker) %>%
  mutate(
    avg_assets = (total_assets + lag(total_assets)) / 2,
    accrual_cfs = (net_income - cfo) / avg_assets
  )
# Low accrual = LONG (sort ascending, long bottom tercile)
# Rebalance: April 1 each year
```

## Gotchas

1. Anomaly partially attenuated post-publication in US; likely stronger in Korea given lower coverage
2. **Sign convention:** LONG low accrual, SHORT high accrual — do NOT flip
3. Loss firms: exclude or treat separately (negative income complicates interpretation)
4. 5-day filing buffer: April 5 not April 1 for safe signal activation
5. Interaction with QMJ: ACC is a sub-component of QMJ Profitability — avoid double-counting if running both
