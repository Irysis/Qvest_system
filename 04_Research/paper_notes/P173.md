# P173: Methodologies Used in the Interactive Smart Beta Tool — Research Affiliates
**Category:** Smart_Beta | **Priority:** 3

## Core Finding
Research Affiliates' technical specification for their Smart Beta tool covering 65+ strategy variants across Value, Income, Low Volatility, Quality, Momentum, Multi-Factor, and Size categories. Key methodological elements: (1) Expected Returns Model: E(r) = alpha_struct + avg(beta_P, beta_group) * z(ln(val_ratio)) — expected return is structural alpha plus a valuation-adjusted component; (2) Structural Alpha = Excess Return - Revaluation Alpha; (3) Trading Cost Model: 0.30% per 10% of ADV traded, capped at 2% price impact; (4) Strategy specifications are fully quantified — Gen-1 Value uses composite of Book/Price + 5yr sales growth + 2yr earnings growth, weighted by P(X) * market_cap * free_float.

## Key Risk/Concern for Korean Implementation
The RA trading cost model (0.30% per 10% ADV) is likely an underestimate for Korea: Korean bid-ask spreads and market impact are 2–3x higher than US. The RA valuation ratio model (pooling betas across regions) cannot be directly applied to Korea because Korea is a single-country market with its own structural characteristics. Applying RA's shrinkage target (average across all regions) would pull Korean factor parameters toward US/global values, potentially mismeasuring the Korean structural alpha.

## Actionable Takeaway
1. Adopt the RA Structural Alpha / Revaluation Alpha decomposition formula directly in the Korean pipeline — it maps cleanly onto the backtest_harness.R framework. Compute: structural_alpha = excess_return - coef(val_ratio_trend) * years_in_sample.
2. Use the RA trading cost model as a lower bound: assume 0.50% per 10% ADV for Korean small/mid-cap stocks (vs. RA's 0.30%) to get conservative net-of-trading-cost performance estimates.
