## Core Thesis

The **q-factor model** — a four-factor model grounded in the investment CAPM — prices 80 anomalies better than FF3 and Carhart (4-factor). Two new factors derived from neoclassical investment theory:
- **r_I/A**: Investment factor (low investment → high returns)
- **r_ROE**: Profitability factor (high ROE → high returns)

The model provides a theory-grounded alternative to the ad hoc empirical factor zoo.

---

## Model Specification

```
E[r_i] = β_MKT·E[MKT] + β_ME·E[r_ME] + β_I/A·E[r_I/A] + β_ROE·E[r_ROE]
```

### Factor Construction:

| Factor | Definition | Rebalancing |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **MKT** | Market excess return | Monthly |
| **r_ME** | Small-minus-Big (size) | Annual, June |
| **r_I/A** | Low-minus-High investment: ΔAT/AT_{t-1} (annual assets) | Annual, June |
| **r_ROE** | High-minus-Low ROE: IBQ / lagged book equity (quarterly EPS) | **Monthly** (key innovation) |

**Critical difference from FF5:** ROE uses **quarterly** earnings with **monthly** rebalancing — captures high-frequency earnings information that annual rebalancing misses.

### Factor Definitions (Appendix A):

**I/A (Investment-to-Assets):**
```
I/A = (AT_t - AT_{t-1}) / AT_{t-1}
```
where AT = total assets (Compustat). Annual rebalancing June t using fiscal year t-1 data.

**ROE (Return on Equity):**
```
ROE = IBQ_q / BE_{q-1}
```
where IBQ = quarterly income before extraordinary items; BE = book equity at end of prior fiscal quarter.
Monthly rebalancing using most recently announced quarterly earnings.

## Main Results

### Factor Performance (US, 1972-2012):

| Factor | Avg Return | t-stat | Sharpe |
|--------|------------|--------|--------|
| r_ME   | 0.31%/mo  | 2.07   | —      |
| r_I/A  | 0.45%/mo  | 4.95   | —      |
| r_ROE  | 0.55%/mo  | 4.82   | —      |

### Model Comparison — Average |alpha| for 80 Anomalies (Table 1):

| Model | Avg |alpha| (%/mo) | Anomalies significant (t>2) |
|-------|------------------|---------------------------|
| FF3   | 0.16             | 57                        |
| Carhart | 0.12           | 41                        |
| **q-factor** | **0.11** | **35**                   |

**Max Sharpe² comparison (Table 11):** q-factor = 0.43 > Carhart = 0.30 > FF3 = 0.21

### The ONE Anomaly q-factor Fails (Table 9):
**OA/POA (Operating Accruals / Piotroski Operating Accruals)** — q-factor cannot fully price accruals anomaly. This suggests accruals capture information beyond investment and profitability (likely earnings quality / manipulation).

### Factor Loadings for Profitability Anomalies:
- ROE loadings: 0.18 to 1.50 for profitability/earnings anomalies
- I/A loadings: -0.65 to -1.37 for investment anomalies
- Momentum loaded heavily on r_ROE (earnings momentum = profitability momentum)

## Appendix A: Key Variable Definitions

### Profitability Variables:
- **SUE** (standardized unexpected earnings): (IBQ_t - IBQ_{t-4}) / std(IBQ_t - IBQ_{t-4}) over 8 quarters
- **RS** (revenue surprise): similar construction using revenue
- **GP** (gross profitability): (REVT - COGS) / AT
- **ROA**: IB / AT (annual income / assets)
- **ROE**: IB / BE (annual income / book equity)
- **TATO**: REVT / AT (asset turnover)

### Investment Variables:
- **AG** (asset growth): ΔAT / AT_{t-1}
- **NOA** (net operating assets): (AT - CHE - (AT - LT - MCEQ - TSTKQ - MIBNQ)) / AT
- **IG** (investment growth): ΔCAPX / CAPX_{t-1}
- **NSI** (net stock issuance): log(split-adjusted shares_t / shares_{t-1})
- **CEI** (composite equity issuance): log(ME_t / ME_{t-5}) - r_{t-5, t}

### Quality/Accrual Variables:
- **Acc** (accruals): ΔCA - ΔCL - ΔCash + ΔSTDebt - Dep; scaled by avg AT
- **OA** (operating accruals): (ΔCA - ΔCash) - (ΔCL - ΔSTD - ΔTP) - Dep; scaled by avg AT
- **POA** (percent operating accruals): OA / |IB|
- **AccQ** (accrual quality): cross-sectional Dechow-Dichev regression residual SD over 5 years — most sophisticated measure; Q1-Q5 spreads in q-factor still significant
- **OL** (operating leverage): (COGS + XSGA) / AT

### R&D Variables:
- **RD/M** (R&D-to-market): XRD / ME (for firms with positive R&D only)
- **RC/A** (R&D capital-to-assets): XRD weighted 5-year sum (20% annual depreciation) / AT
  - RC = 0.2×XRD_t + 0.4×XRD_{t-1} + 0.6×XRD_{t-2} + 0.8×XRD_{t-3} + XRD_{t-4}

### Trading Friction Variables (Appendix A.6):
- **ME**: log(market equity)
- **Ivol**: idiosyncratic volatility = std(residuals from FF3) over trailing month (daily)
- **Tvol**: total return volatility (trailing month daily)
- **Svol**: systematic volatility = Tvol² - Ivol²
- **MDR**: max daily return in trailing month (Bali et al. 2011)
- **β** (Frazzini-Pedersen): β̂ = ρ̂ · (σ̂_i / σ̂_m); ρ̂ from 5yr monthly, σ̂ from 1yr daily
- **D-β** (Dimson 1979): sum of lead+lag+contemporaneous market betas (handles thin trading)
- **S-Rev**: short-term reversal = month t-1 return
- **Disp**: IBES EPS forecast dispersion = std(analyst EPS forecasts) / |mean forecast|
- **Turn**: 6-month average daily share turnover
- **1/P**: reciprocal of nominal stock price
- **Dvol**: average daily dollar volume over 6 months
- **Illiq**: Amihud (2002) = avg(|r_d| / Dvol_d) over trailing year

## Korean Implementation

**Data mapping for q-factor in Korean context:**

| q-Factor | Korean Data Source | DART Field |
|----------|-------------------|------------|
| r_I/A | DART 재무제표 | 자산총계 YoY change |
| r_ROE | DART 분기보고서 | 당기순이익 / (자본총계_{t-1}) |
| r_ME | OHLCVS market cap | 시가총액 |

**Korean-specific adjustments:**
1. **Quarterly ROE**: 분기보고서 (Q1: May, Q2: Aug, Q3: Nov, Q4: Apr) — monthly rebalancing feasible but lagged by DART reporting
2. **I/A**: Use 연결재무제표 only (chaebol cross-holdings inflate standalone assets)
3. **ROE denominator**: Use 지배기업 지분 자본 (controlling shareholders' equity) for chaebol
4. **Universe breakpoints**: 30th/70th on KOSPI200+KOSDAQ150 separately (not NYSE-style)
5. **q-factor vs FF5**: ROE monthly rebalancing gives edge in Korea where quarterly earnings releases are informative events

**Strategy utility for STR_013:**
- r_ROE provides a *theory-grounded* alternative to QMJ's Profitability composite
- Monthly ROE rebalancing is the key advantage over annual-rebalanced FF3/FF5 factors
- Combine: use q-factor ROE as one input to profitability composite alongside GPOA

**Regime conditioning:**
- r_I/A (low investment premium): strongest in late-cycle (capex slowdown precedes ROE decline)
- r_ROE: strongest during recovery (earnings rebound mispriced)
