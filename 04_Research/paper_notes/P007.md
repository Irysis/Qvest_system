# P007: Path-Dependence of Leveraged ETF Returns (2010)

## Core Finding
LETFs have a provable negative exposure to the realized variance of the underlying index. The exact return formula is: log(L_t/L_0) ≈ β * log(S_t/S_0) - (β²-β)/2 * realized_variance, where β is the leverage ratio. Empirically validated on 56 LETFs (44 double-levered, 12 triple-levered) using daily data from inception through March 2009. Rebalancing frequency of ~1 week is needed to maintain target leverage exposure.

## Key Metrics
- Alpha/Return: Quarterly underperformance of LETFs vs. static leveraged strategy is substantial in high-vol periods; formula fits empirical data with excellent agreement
- Statistical strength: Tested on 56 funds across all available history from January 2008
- Sample: US leveraged ETF universe, Jan 2008 - Mar 2009

## Korea Applicability
MEDIUM — Direct applicability to KODEX/TIGER leveraged ETF products. The mathematical formula for volatility drag is universal. Korean VKOSPI (volatility index) can proxy realized variance input.

## Strategy Implication
For any strategy using levered instruments, compute the drag explicitly: drag = (beta^2 - beta)/2 * realized_var_annualized. In R: drag <- (beta^2 - beta) / 2 * var(daily_returns) * 252. This drag must be subtracted from expected gross alpha in the hurdle gate. In a high-volatility macro regime (VIX > 25 in macro_fred.parquet), the drag can exceed 5-10% annually, making leveraged positions uneconomic.
