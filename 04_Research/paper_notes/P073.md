# P073: Crowded Trades, Short Covering, and Momentum Crashes (Yan 2014 — Princeton PhD Thesis, Chapter 1)
**Category:** Factor_Return / Crowding
**Feasibility:** medium (requires institutional ownership data; DART quarterly filings available in Korea)
**Novelty:** high (best mechanistic explanation of momentum crashes)

## Core Claim
Momentum crashes are caused by short covering in crowded loser stocks during market rebounds. A double sort on Short Interest Ratio (SIR) × Institutional Exit Rate (EXIT) identifies "crowded loser" stocks — stocks that are heavily shorted AND being actively exited by institutions. A momentum strategy that excludes crowded losers avoids crashes while retaining most of the momentum premium.

## Crowding Detection Method (exact metrics if available)

**Primary Crowding Measure (stock-level):**
- **SIR (Short Interest Ratio)**: shares sold short / shares outstanding (monthly, from COMPUSTAT/CRSP)
- **EXIT (Institutional Exit Rate)**: % of institutional investors (13F filers) that exited the stock in the past quarter = (# funds holding at t-1 that do NOT hold at t) / (# funds holding at t-1)

**Double sort construction:**
- Sort stocks independently into terciles by SIR and EXIT
- **Crowded Losers** = stocks in TOP tercile of BOTH SIR and EXIT (high short interest + institutions fleeing)
- **Non-crowded Losers** = bottom tercile of either SIR or EXIT

**Momentum strategy modification:**
- Standard WML: Long top decile 12m-1m momentum, Short bottom decile 12m-1m momentum
- Non-crowded WML: Same long leg, but short leg excludes crowded losers (replacing with non-crowded losers)
- Non-crowded WML avoids momentum crashes because crowded losers reverse sharply during rebounds

**Crash mechanism**: During market upturns, short sellers must cover crowded losers first (highest cost to maintain short), causing disproportionate price appreciation in loser stocks → momentum portfolio's short leg loses → momentum crash.

## Key Results
- Non-crowded momentum strategy: avoids all major momentum crashes (Jan 2001, Mar-May 2009)
- Annualized alpha improvement: +4–6% from removing crowded losers
- Crowded loser identification (SIR × EXIT) is 1-quarter lagged but still predictive
- EXIT rate is more informative than SIR alone: institutions exiting = informed departure signal
- Strategy works across US large-cap, mid-cap; also documented in international markets (partially)
- Crowded loser stocks have: high short interest, declining institutional breadth, moderate beta, high volatility

## Korea Applicability
- **SIR proxy**: KRX 공매도 잔고 (short balance) / 상장주식수 (shares outstanding) — available daily at stock level. Excellent substitute for SIR.
- **EXIT proxy**: DART 대량보유보고 (5%+ holder disclosures) — only for major shareholders. Mass EXIT signal requires quarterly institutional holdings, which is NOT available in Korea (no Korean 13F equivalent).
  - **Alternative**: Use quarterly changes in 외국인 지분율 (foreigner ownership ratio) from KRX as EXIT proxy. Foreigners = quasi-institutional smart money in Korea.
  - **Alternative 2**: Use DART quarterly reports + changes in institutional ownership block from fund filings (간접투자 펀드 보고).
- **Momentum limitation**: STR_011 is LowVol, not Momentum. However, during market rebounds, LowVol stocks may also face crowded unwinding.
- **Key adaptation**: Apply SIR × foreigner EXIT double sort to identify crowded LowVol stocks (low-vol stocks with high short interest AND declining foreigner ownership) → exclude from portfolio.
