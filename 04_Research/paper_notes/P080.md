# P080: Replicating Anomalies (Hou, Xue, Zhang 2020)
**Category:** Anomaly_Research / Replication
**Relevance to Korea pipeline:** Critical

## Core Claim
The majority of published stock market anomalies fail to replicate when methodology is corrected for microcap bias. Using NYSE breakpoints and value-weighted returns (the gold standard), 65% of 452 anomalies fail even a single significance test (t < 1.96). The anomaly literature has been systematically inflated by equal-weighting and market-cap breakpoints that over-represent tiny, illiquid stocks.

## Key Findings (specific percentages, survival rates, which anomalies)
- **452 anomalies** tested across 6 categories
- **65% failure rate** under NYSE breakpoints + value-weighted returns (the "correct" method)
- Category-level replication rates (% that SURVIVE):
  - Momentum: **62.2%** (highest survival — 28 of ~45 survive)
  - Investment: **73.7%** (second highest — robust category)
  - Value-vs-Growth: **42%** (moderate survival)
  - Profitability: **44.3%** (moderate survival)
  - Intangibles: **25.2%** (mostly fails — driven by microcap)
  - Trading Frictions: **3.8%** (almost total failure — nearly all microcap artifacts)
- Key mechanism: microcap stocks (bottom 20% by NYSE market cap) are 60%+ of EW portfolios by count but only 3% of total market value
- Statistical correction matters: mean t-stat drops from ~3.5 (EW/market breakpoints) to ~2.0 (VW/NYSE breakpoints)
- Survival threshold used: t ≥ 1.96 (5% level, one-sided)

## Factors That SURVIVE Post-Publication
- **Momentum** (most robust category, 62% survive)
- **Investment factor** (asset growth, investment-to-assets — 74% survive, strongest category)
- **Profitability** (gross profitability, ROA, ROE — ~44% survive)
- **Value** (B/M, E/P, CF/P — ~42% survive)
- Core Fama-French factors (market, size, value) survive by construction

## Factors That DECAY Post-Publication
- **Trading Frictions anomalies** (bid-ask spread, illiquidity, turnover effects): 96.2% FAIL — essentially all artifacts of microcap bias
- **Intangibles anomalies** (R&D, patents, advertising intensity): 74.8% FAIL — concentrated in small, illiquid firms
- Most EW-only anomalies in profitability and value subcategories
- Many technical/chart-based signals fail

## Korea/International Applicability
- Directly applicable to Korean universe design: KOSPI200 + KOSDAQ150 universe already filters microcaps, which means the surviving factor set is MORE appropriate
- NYSE breakpoint methodology = use Korean market median (KOSPI/KOSDAQ combined median) as breakpoints
- Investment factor survival (73.7%) is particularly strong signal: asset growth, capex/assets, accruals should be in pipeline
- Trading frictions / illiquidity factors: KOSPI200 universe is liquid enough that these become microcap-free, potentially improving survival vs US full-market tests
- Warning: do NOT test factors on full Korean market universe — restrict to top 350 stocks minimum

## Actionable Implications for Our Pipeline
1. **Prioritize Investment + Momentum + Profitability factors** — these have highest replication rates
2. **Avoid Trading Frictions category** entirely (3.8% survival) unless testing on liquid universe
3. **Use value-weighted returns** in all backtests (already done in harness)
4. **Use market-cap based breakpoints** rather than equal-split quantiles for factor construction
5. **Intangibles factors** (R&D intensity, patent value) need micro-cap clean universe to be valid
6. Cross-check every new factor: does it survive value-weighted test with size-neutral portfolios?
7. For strategy design: **Investment + Profitability composite** is most replication-robust combination

## Notes
- Paper: Hou K., Xue C., Zhang L. (2020), "Replicating Anomalies", Review of Financial Studies 33(5), pp. 2019-2133
- This is the definitive "anomaly graveyard" paper — essential reading before any new factor is added
- Companion q-factor model: market, size, investment, profitability (ROE) — 4 factors
- Specific anomalies mentioned as surviving: SUE (earnings surprise), RS (revenue surprise), RE (return on equity), IA (investment-to-assets), ROA, GP (gross profitability)
- Specific anomalies that fail: most volume-based, most liquidity-based, most intangible-based
