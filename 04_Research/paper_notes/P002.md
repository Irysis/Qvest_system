# P002: Appendix B — Three Fundamental Models of Stock Returns (2022)

## Core Finding
Three valuation frameworks underpin QEPM stock selection: (1) Dividend Discount Model (DDM) — single and two-stage variants pricing stock as PDV of future dividends; (2) residual income / abnormal earnings models; (3) multifactor APT models. The DDM provides a tractable signal for ranking stocks by implied growth vs. market-implied growth rate.

## Korea Applicability
MEDIUM — DDM applicability is limited in Korea due to low and irregular dividend payout culture among KOSPI/KOSDAQ firms. However, implied-growth-rate ranking (g = (Pr - D0)/(D + P)) is implementable using DART dividend data and provides a fundamental valuation signal.

## Strategy Implication
Compute implied DDM growth rate for each stock in the universe using DART fundamental data (D0 = dividends per share, r = required return estimated via CAPM). Rank stocks by (implied_growth - consensus_growth): positive spread = undervalued. Can be used as a value tilt factor in a composite score. Two-stage DDM applicable for KOSDAQ growth names.
