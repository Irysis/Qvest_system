# P018: Opinions and Social Pressure (1955)

## Core Finding
Asch's landmark conformity experiments show that 75% of subjects conform at least once to an obviously incorrect group consensus when answering simple perceptual questions (line length comparison). When alone, error rate is near zero; with 3+ confederates giving wrong answers, error rate rises to ~37% on critical trials. Even one dissenter dramatically reduces conformity — unanimity is critical to social pressure effects.

## Key Metrics
- Alpha/Return: N/A (social psychology experiment)
- Statistical strength: ~75% conformity rate on at least one trial; 37% average error rate on critical trials
- Sample: US college students, Scientific American 1955

This creates momentum cascades and subsequent reversals that are larger than in Western markets.
