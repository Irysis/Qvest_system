## Key Content Confirmed in Pages 1-20

The Springer published version confirms identical content to P135 and P184 (SSRN draft):

### Quality Score Framework (same as P135/P184):
```
Quality = z(Profitability + Growth + Safety)

Profitability = z(GPOA + ROE + ROA + CFOA + GMAR + ACC)
Growth        = z(Δ5yr_GPOA + Δ5yr_ROE + Δ5yr_ROA + Δ5yr_CFOA + Δ5yr_GMAR)
Safety        = z(-Beta - Leverage - O_score - Z_distress - EVOL)
```

### Key Results Confirmed (same as P135/P184):
- US 4-factor alpha: 1.05%/mo (t=9.31) — identical across all three versions
- Global 4-factor alpha: 0.99%/mo (t=6.67)
- Price of Quality slope: b=0.22 US, 0.17 global
- QMJ beta: negative (-0.36 to -0.59) — defensive factor
