## Summary (abbreviated)

**Core Thesis**: Fama-MacBeth develop the two-pass cross-sectional regression procedure and find that NYSE data (1935-1968) cannot reject the two-parameter CAPM: beta is the only priced risk measure, return-beta relationship is linear, non-beta risk is unpriced. Zero-beta return exceeds risk-free rate (Black 1972 confirmed).

**Methodology**: Monthly cross-sectional regression of portfolio returns on lagged beta, beta^2, and non-beta risk (s(e)). Time-series inference on monthly gamma estimates. Key equation:
```
R_pt = gamma_0t + gamma_1t*beta_p,t-1 + gamma_2t*beta^2_p,t-1 + gamma_3t*s_p,t-1(e) + eta_pt
```

**Key Numbers** (1935-6/68):
- gamma_1 (risk premium): mean = 0.0085/month, t = 2.57 (positive risk-return tradeoff confirmed)
- gamma_2 (nonlinearity): t = 0.02 (linearity not rejected)
- gamma_3 (non-beta risk): t = -0.29 (non-beta risk not priced)
- gamma_0 - r_f: mean = 0.0048/month, t = 3.24 (zero-beta premium confirmed)
