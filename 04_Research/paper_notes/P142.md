## Core Thesis

Tests profitability and value factors across **international markets** including Asia Pacific, Europe, and North America. Key findings:
- **GP/ME** (gross profitability / market equity) is the strongest single profitability measure internationally
- Profitability premium is **globally robust** but varies significantly by region
- **Asia Pacific** shows particularly strong profitability effects, especially for large-cap stocks

## Korean Market Implications

**This is the most directly relevant international study for Korea:**

1. **Asia Pacific large-cap FFC alpha = 1.34%/mo** — Korea is in the Asia Pacific region. The profitability premium is STRONGEST for big stocks in this region.

2. **GP/ME > GP/A for Korea**: Scale gross profit by market cap, not total assets. KOSPI200 large-cap firms: use GP/ME as primary signal (better than GP/A).
   ```
   GP/ME = (매출액 - 매출원가) / 시가총액
   ```

3. **Large-cap focus justified**: Unlike US where profitability is stronger in small-caps, Asia Pacific shows the reverse — KOSPI200 focus is correct.

4. **OP/ME also strong** in Asia Pacific: Consider operating profit version alongside gross profit version in composite.

5. **Japan similarity**: Korean and Japanese market structures share many features (keiretsu ↔ chaebol, dividend culture, cross-holdings). Japan results (ROE strongest = 0.89%/mo FFC alpha) suggest ROE may be particularly relevant for Korean chaebol analysis.

**Enhanced Korea profitability composite (incorporating Cakici findings):**
```r
# Korea profitability composite — Asia Pacific optimized
korea_quality <- dart_fund %>%
  mutate(
    # GP/ME — strongest Asia Pacific signal (weight 2x)
    gp_me = gross_profit / market_cap,
    # GP/A — standard gross profitability (weight 1x)
    gp_a  = gross_profit / total_assets,
    # OP/ME — operating profitability / market (weight 1.5x)
    op_me = operating_profit / market_cap,
    # Composite (Asia Pacific-calibrated weights)
    profitability_kor = 2 * z_rank(gp_me) +
                        1 * z_rank(gp_a)  +
                       1.5 * z_rank(op_me)
  )
```

**Regime conditioning:**
- Asia Pacific profitability premium may be more regime-stable than North America
- During EM stress periods (KRW depreciation, China slowdown): profitability premium tends to compress as quality stocks are sold for liquidity
- Weight DOWN during KRW crisis periods (see FRED: DEXKOUS regime signals)
