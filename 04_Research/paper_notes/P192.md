# Paper Note: P192 — Breadth Momentum and Vigilance Asset Allocation: BAA (2022)

**Authors:** Wouter J. Keller, Jan Willem Keuning
**Source:** SSRN Working Paper (2022)
**Category:** Asset_Allocation
**Priority:** 1

## Core Thesis

BAA (Breadth Adaptive Asset Allocation) is the fourth generation in Keller's tactical allocation family (PAA→VAA→DAA→BAA). BAA combines two momentum signals: a slow relative momentum signal (SMA12-based, for asset selection) and a fast absolute momentum signal (13612W-based, for crash protection via canary assets). The canary universe (SPY, VWO, VEA, BND) serves as a leading indicator — if any canary has negative 13612W momentum, the strategy shifts partially or fully to cash assets.

## Strategy Description

**Two-Signal Architecture:**

**Signal 1 — Slow (Relative Momentum, for asset selection):**
- Formula: MOM12 = p0/SMA(12) - 1
- Simple 12-month price vs. SMA12 comparison
- Used to rank and select top T assets from offensive universe

**Signal 2 — Fast (Absolute Momentum, for protection):**
- Formula: 13612W = 12*(p0/p1) + 4*(p0/p3) + 2*(p0/p6) + 1*(p0/p12) - 19
- Applied to canary universe: SPY, VWO, VEA, BND (4 assets)
- Count canary assets with negative 13612W: b_canary (0 to 4)
- If b_canary = 0: 100% offensive assets
- If b_canary = 1: CF = 1/T of cash, rest in top assets
- If b_canary >= 2 (B=1 setting): 100% defensive

**Cash Universe (pick best by MOM12):** SHY, IEF, LQD

**Best Configurations:**
- BAA-G12 (T=6, B=2): Offensive universe = SPY,IWM,QQQ,VGK,EWJ,EEM,VNQ,DJP,GLD,TLT,HYG,LQD
  - Full Sample (Dec 1970 - Aug 2022): R=14.6%, V=8.7%, D=8.7%, SR=1.19, MAR=1.69
- BAA-G4 (T=1, B=1): Offensive universe = SPY,VEA,VWO,BND
  - Full Sample: R=21.0%, V=12.6%, D=14.6%, SR=1.21, MAR=1.44

**Canary mechanism advantage:** Canary assets (SPY, VWO, VEA, BND) are more diversified than VAA's single-universe approach, providing earlier crash detection without excessive false positives.

## Key Results

- BAA-G12 SR=1.19 vs VAA-G12 SR=0.85 — significant improvement
- BAA-G4 achieves SR=1.21 with simple 4-asset universe
- Canary B=1 threshold (any canary negative → full defensive) optimal for crash protection
- Separation of selection signal (slow SMA12) and protection signal (fast 13612W) is key innovation
- Lower turnover than VAA due to slow selection signal
- Consistent performance across all sub-periods since 1970
- Max DD consistently below 10-15% vs buy-and-hold equity ~50%

## Implementation in Korean Market

**Canary assets (Korean proxies):**
- SPY → KODEX 200TR or TIGER 미국S&P500
- VWO → TIGER 신흥국MSCI
- VEA → KODEX 선진국MSCI World
- BND → TIGER 미국채10년선물

**Offensive universe adaptation:**
- Replace US-centric ETFs with Korean-accessible global ETFs
- Include KOSPI (코스피200) as offensive asset
- Keep international diversification: EAFE, EM, bonds, gold

**Connection to existing infrastructure:**
- 13612W canary signal directly comparable to Buddha_Mode in macro_regime.parquet
- BAA-G4 is simpler and potentially better than MVAA for Korean implementation
- `RAWDATA.parquet` can provide proxy price series for backtesting

**Feasibility: VERY HIGH** — simpler than MVAA, superior performance metrics
