# Paper Note: P043 — Larson, Sloan & Giedt (2018)
# "Defining, measuring, and modeling accruals: a guide for researchers"

**Journal:** Review of Accounting Studies (2018)
**Category:** Factor_Return / Accrual_Factors
**Priority:** 1

## Core Thesis
Comprehensive methodological guide: unified accrual definitions, decomposition into major classes, modeling guidance. NOT a trading strategy paper — foundational reference.

## Key Contribution
**Comprehensive accruals (COMPACC):** COMPACC = ΔCEQ - ΔCHE (change in equity minus change in cash)
**Decompositions:**
- OPACC (operating) vs. FINACC (financial)
- WCACC (working capital) vs. LTACC (long-term)

**Key insight:** Different accrual types have vastly different persistence and reversal speed:
- Working capital accruals: reverse quickly (1-2 years)
- Long-term operating accruals (LTACC): persist longer → stronger long-term signal

**Growth adjustment:** ACC_t = α + α₁EMPGR_t + α₂(EMPGR_t × NCI_{t-1}) + α₃CF_t
