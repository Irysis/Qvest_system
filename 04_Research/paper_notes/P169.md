# P169: Smart Beta Strategies in Crypto Asset Investment (2018)
**Category:** Smart_Beta | **Priority:** 2

## Core Finding
893 crypto assets, June 2014–June 2018. Size: large-cap outperforms small-cap (REVERSE of equity markets) — large Sharpe 0.61 vs. small 0.28. Low volatility works strongly: BAV Sharpe 0.69 vs. high-vol Sharpe -1.18 (t = -2.36). Momentum works at all horizons (14-day winners: 8.58%/month vs. losers: -4.12%). Value proxies (nodes-to-market, volume-to-market) show no clear monotonic pattern. Long-only factors highly correlated with market: BIG 0.91, LOW 0.93, WIN 0.81 — long-only crypto factor returns are primarily market beta, not factor alpha.

## Key Risk/Concern for Korean Implementation
This paper is primarily a reference point rather than a direct implementation guide for the KOSPI200/KOSDAQ150 pipeline. The key transferable insight: even in highly immature, informationally inefficient markets (crypto 2014–2018), momentum and low-vol premiums are detectable, but value proxies based on fundamentals do not. This validates momentum and low-vol as the most robust "primitive" factors, while also confirming that value requires fundamental data quality unavailable in nascent markets.

## Actionable Takeaway
1. The large-cap outperformance in crypto (reverse of equities) reflects market maturity effects — in illiquid/immature markets, large cap may dominate due to survival bias and liquidity premia. This is a warning against direct transplantation of US small-cap premium logic to highly illiquid KOSDAQ small caps.
2. The high market correlation of long-only factor portfolios (0.81–0.93) confirms that long-only factor strategies in any concentrated market are predominantly market beta plays; to isolate genuine factor alpha, long-short or market-neutral construction is required.
