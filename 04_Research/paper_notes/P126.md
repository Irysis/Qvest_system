## Summary (see P134.md for full details)
- Confirms variance-sorted volatility anomaly in U.S. (Q1 $136.21 vs Q5 $0.02) and 21 global markets
- KEY FINDING: When sorting on RELATIVE VARIANCE (downside semi-variance component), the anomaly REVERSES — confirming the standard risk-return trade-off
- Interpretation: The long-term volatility anomaly is primarily a COMPOUNDING/VARIANCE DRAG effect, not genuine mispricing of symmetric risk
- Downside risk IS compensated; symmetric variance is NOT
- Practical implication for STR_011: use relative variance as a RISK SCREEN (exclude high downside-risk stocks), not as the primary sorting variable
- Beta shrinkage formula: β^adj = 0.4 + 0.6 * β^ts (Vasicek/Frazzini-Pedersen approach)
- 21 developed market evidence confirms global robustness with variance sort
