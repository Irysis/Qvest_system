# P055 — Shi & Zhang (2011)

**File:** accrual8.pdf
**Citation:** Shi, L., Zhang, H. (2011). On alternative measures of accruals. *Accounting Horizons*, 25(4), 811–836.

---

## Core Claim

The two most commonly used accrual measures — balance sheet accruals (ACCR_BS) and cash-flow statement total accruals (TACCR_CF) — differ not only due to non-articulation events (Hribar & Collins 2002) but also due to four additional items in TACCR_CF not in ACCR_BS, and due to non-articulations in working capital accounts and depreciation. After cleaning for non-articulation events, ACCR_BS yields higher accrual strategy returns (8.3% vs 7.0% annually) than ACCR_CF. The dominant driver is "other funds from operations" (OF) — a special-item-like component that is mispriced to a lesser extent than regular accruals.

---

## Factor Definition

**Three Accrual Measures Compared:**

**1. Balance Sheet Accruals (ACCR_BS) — Sloan 1996:**
```
ACCR_BS = [(ΔCA_BS - ΔCASH_BS) - (ΔCL_BS - ΔSTD_BS) - Dep_IS] / Avass
```

**2. Cash-Flow Statement Operating Accruals (ACCR_CF):**
```
ACCR_CF = [ΔAR_CF + ΔINV_CF - ΔAP_CF - ΔTP_CF + ΔOT_CF - ΔDEP_CF] / Avass
```
(All items from statement of cash flows: RECCH, INVCH, APALCH, TXACH, AOLOCH, DPC)

**3. Cash-Flow Statement Total Accruals (TACCR_CF):**
```
TACCR_CF = (Earnings - CFO) / Avass
```
Where CFO = OANCF - XIDOC (extraordinary items and disc. ops)

**Four Items in TACCR_CF but NOT in ACCR_CF (and not in ACCR_BS):**
- DEF_TAX: Deferred income tax benefit/expense (TXDI)
- EQU_GL: Equity in net earnings/losses of unconsolidated affiliates
- PPE_GL: Gains/losses from sales of PPE and investments
- OF: Other funds from operations (residual special items bucket)

**Non-Articulations (differences between BS and CF measures):**
- DIF_AR: AR difference between B/S and SCF (60% of firms have non-zero DIF_AR)
- DIF_INV, DIF_AP, DIF_TP, DIF_OT, DIF_DEP: similar for other WC items
- Combined non-articulation magnitude: 6.4% of average total assets

**Strategy Returns (sample 1988–2007, 50,196 obs, non-articulation events excluded):**
| Measure | Annual Strategy Return | Significance |
|---------|----------------------|--------------|
| ACCR_BS | 8.3% | p < 0.01 |
| ACCR_CF | 7.0% | p < 0.05 |
| TACCR_CF | 4.1% | p = n.s. |

**Key finding:** OF (other funds from operations) is responsible for most of the TACCR_CF vs ACCR_CF return differential. OF is mispriced to a lesser extent — consistent with special items being more transparent to investors (Burgstahler et al. 2002).

## Key Empirical Results

- Sample: NYSE/AMEX/NASDAQ, 1988–2007, 50,196 firm-year observations (after non-articulation exclusions)
- Non-articulation affects 60%+ of firms — surprisingly prevalent
- Main causes of non-articulation: (1) special events (write-offs, asset sales); (2) Compustat errors; (3) firms' inconsistent definitions; (4) non-standard asset/liability classifications
- ACCR_BS > ACCR_CF > TACCR_CF in strategy returns (monotone ordering)
- OF component coefficient is positive and significant (+0.259 in Panel A, +0.119 in Panel B)
- DIF_AR (accounts receivable non-articulation) is responsible for most of the ACCR_BS > ACCR_CF differential
- Fama-MacBeth regression confirms: ACCR_CF coefficient = −0.070 to −0.119 (significant); TACCR_CF coefficient = −0.041 to not significant

## Korea Applicability

- **High for practical implementation guidance** — directly addresses which accrual measure to use in Korea
- K-IFRS adopters (2011+) report both balance sheet and statement of cash flows — ACCR_BS and ACCR_CF are both computable
- DART provides both sets of data items in `fundamental_dart.parquet`
- **Recommendation for Korea:** Use ACCR_BS (balance sheet measure) rather than earnings-minus-CFO as the primary accrual signal, consistent with this paper's finding that ACCR_BS has higher strategy returns
- Non-articulation events in Korea: M&A activity (especially post-2015 corporate restructuring) creates non-articulation; filter out firm-years with major acquisitions
- The DEF_TAX and EQU_GL components are especially large for Korean chaebol (large deferred tax provisions, extensive equity method investment in affiliates)

## Actionable Strategy Ideas

1. **Measure Selection Guidance — Confirm ACCR_BS for STR_012:** This paper validates that STR_012's balance-sheet-based measure is the RIGHT choice (higher strategy returns than CFS-based). No change needed to the existing STR_012 factor definition.

2. **Non-Articulation Screening Filter:** Add a filter to exclude observations with large non-articulations in accounts receivable (DIF_AR > threshold):

```r
# Non-articulation screening
# If both BS and SCF data are available in DART
accrual_clean <- fundamentals %>%
  mutate(
    # Balance sheet AR change
    dar_bs = rect - lag(rect),
    # SCF AR change (from cash flow statement)
    dar_cf = -recch,  # DART: recch = decrease in receivables on SCF
    # Non-articulation indicator
    dif_ar = dar_bs - dar_cf,
    # Flag large non-articulations (>2% of assets)
    flag_nonartic = abs(dif_ar / at) > 0.02
  ) %>%
  # Filter option A: exclude flagged observations
  filter(!flag_nonartic)
  # Filter option B: winsorize instead
```

3. **Four-Item Decomposition Signal:** Separately extract the four items that distinguish TACCR_CF from ACCR_CF. The DEF_TAX and EQU_GL items are independently interesting for Korean chaebol analysis:
   - High DEF_TAX (large deferred tax benefit) = aggressive tax accounting
   - High EQU_GL (equity method losses absorbed) = affiliate distress signal

```r
# Korean chaebol-specific: equity method investment accruals
# EQU_GL equivalent in K-IFRS
equ_gl_signal <- fundamentals %>%
  mutate(
    # Equity method income (positive = affiliates profitable)
    # Negative = affiliates losing money, absorbed as accrual
    equ_income = equity_method_income / at,
    # Deferred tax: rising DTA = aggressive earnings recognition
    def_tax_accrual = (deferred_tax_asset - lag(deferred_tax_asset)) / at
  )
```
