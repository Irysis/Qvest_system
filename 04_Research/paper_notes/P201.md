## Core Thesis

This paper introduces MDAA (Meritz Defensive Asset Allocation) as a new fourth strategy alongside the existing 3 Meritz models (Risk Parity, Dual Momentum, MVAA). MDAA addresses the primary weakness of MVAA — its use of 20-year US Treasuries (미국채20) as the safe asset makes it vulnerable to rising interest rate environments. MDAA solves this by: (1) using only 2 canary assets (신흥국 주식, 신흥국 채권 indices) to determine safe asset weight, and (2) replacing 미국채20 with 1~3 year US Treasuries (미국채1~3년) as the safe asset.

## Strategy Description

**MDAA Core Architecture:**

**Canary System (2-asset breadth, simplified vs MVAA's 16):**
- Monitor only 2 assets: EM Equity (신흥국 주식) + EM Bond (신흥국 채권)
- Count how many have negative price momentum: b (0, 1, or 2)
- Safe asset weight: b/2
  - b=0 → 0% safe asset (100% risk assets)
  - b=1 → 50% safe asset
  - b=2 → 100% safe asset

**Safe Asset: 1~3 Year US Treasuries (미국채1~3년 = SHY equivalent)**
- Key difference from MVAA (which uses TLT, 20-year)
- 1~3 year bonds have minimal interest rate duration risk
- During rising rate environment: 미국채1~3년 nearly flat vs 미국채20 falls sharply

**Risk Asset Selection:**
- Universe: same 16 assets as MVAA (코스피, S&P500, EAFE, EM, 나스닥100, 러셀2000, etc.)
- Select Top 4 by momentum (same 13612W or 1,3,6,12 weighted average)
- Equal weight among Top 4 risk assets

**Momentum Evaluation:**
- Same 1,3,6,12 month return weighted average as MVAA
- 12:4:2:1 weighting (same as 13612W from VAA family)
- Applied monthly, end of month

**Portfolio Composition (historical average, 2002-2020):**
- Stocks (주식): ~47%
- Short-term bonds (채권, 1~3년): ~39% (of which 27% is the defined safe asset portion)
- Alternatives (금, 부동산): ~14%
- vs MVAA: 주식 32%, 채권 61% (more stock-heavy but with shorter bonds)

**Full Sample Performance (2001.12 - 2020.11):**
- MDAA: CAGR ~12-13%, S&P500: ~8%
- MDAA Max DD: ~-15% vs S&P500 Max DD: ~-50%
- MDAA vs MVAA: MDAA slightly better return, similar or better drawdown
- 2020 performance: MDAA +21% (best of 4 models)
  - Feb 2020: reduced stock weight to 12.5% (early warning from EM signals)
  - March-April: 0% stocks → avoided COVID crash
  - July+: rapidly increased stocks to 75%+ (faster recovery than MVAA due to different breadth signal)

**Risk/Return Comparison (그림87, 2002-2020):**
- MDAA: ~12.5% return, ~9% vol → best position on risk/return chart
- MVAA: ~11% return, ~10% vol
- Dual Momentum: ~9.5% return, ~8% vol
- Risk Parity: ~7.5% return, ~6% vol
- S&P500: ~8% return, ~15% vol

**16-Asset Universe (same as MVAA in P190):**
KOSPI, S&P500, ACWI ex US, EAFE, EM, NASDAQ100, Russell 2000, EM Bond, 미국채10, 미국채20, 한국채35, 한국채10, Gold, WTI, Real Estate, USD Dollar Index equivalent

## Key Results

- MDAA achieves better Sharpe and lower drawdown than MVAA by replacing 20Y bonds with short-term bonds
- The 2-asset canary (EM equity + EM bond) is sufficient and arguably better than 16-asset breadth
  - EM assets are leading indicators: they deteriorate before developed markets
  - Simpler → less noise
- 2020 COVID performance: MDAA +21% vs MVAA +17%
  - MDAA's 2-asset canary detected risk faster than MVAA's 16-asset breadth at lower threshold
  - Recovery also faster (50% stock weight restored in July vs August for MVAA)
- In rising rate environment (2022 scenario, motivation for strategy):
  - MDAA: 1~3Y bonds barely affected by rate rise
  - MVAA: 20Y bond losses amplify drawdown
- MDAA is the recommended strategy for rate-rising macro environments
- Korean-accessible ETF proxy for 미국채1~3년: TIGER 미국채1~3년선물

## Implementation in Korean Market

**Complete implementation recipe:**

**Step 1: Canary evaluation (monthly)**
```r
# 2 canary assets: EM equity + EM bond (or Korean proxies)
# Use 13612W momentum score
canary_score_em_equity <- 12*(p0/p1) + 4*(p0/p3) + 2*(p0/p6) + 1*(p0/p12) - 19
canary_score_em_bond   <- 12*(p0/p1) + 4*(p0/p3) + 2*(p0/p6) + 1*(p0/p12) - 19
b <- sum(canary_score_em_equity < 0, canary_score_em_bond < 0)  # 0, 1, or 2
safe_weight <- b / 2  # 0%, 50%, or 100%
```

**Step 2: Risk asset selection**
```r
# Evaluate 13612W for all 16 assets
# Select Top 4 by 13612W score
# Equal weight among Top 4 at (1 - safe_weight)
risk_weight <- (1 - safe_weight) / 4  # for each of Top 4 assets
```

**Step 3: Safe asset**
```r
# Korean proxy: TIGER 미국채1~3년선물 (A305080) or SHY
# Or Korean 1~3Y bond ETF: KODEX 단기채권PLUS
```

**Korean ETF Universe:**
- KOSPI: KODEX 200TR

- EAFE: KODEX 선진국MSCI World
- EM Equity (canary + universe): TIGER 신흥국MSCI
- EM Bond (canary): KODEX 신흥국채권
- 나스닥100: TIGER 미국나스닥100
