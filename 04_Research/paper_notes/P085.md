# P085: Foundations of Factor Investing (MSCI Research Insight, December 2013)
**Category:** Factor_Return / Practitioner_Guide
**Relevance to Korea pipeline:** High

## Core Claim
Six systematic risk premia factors — Value, Low Size, Momentum, Low Volatility, Dividend Yield, and Quality — deliver persistent excess returns over the long run in global equity markets. Each factor has dual support from both risk-based AND behavioral explanations. All factors are cyclical (2-3+ year underperformance periods are normal). Growth and Liquidity factors do NOT generate persistent long-run premia.

## Key Findings (specific percentages, survival rates, which anomalies)
- **MSCI World Factor Indexes, June 1988 to June 2013** (25-year live track record):
  - MSCI World benchmark: Total Return 7.2%, Risk 15.3%
  - **Quality**: Total Return 10.9%, Active Return vs benchmark +3.8%, Risk 13.8%
  - **Momentum**: Total Return 10.4%, Active Return +3.3%, Annual Turnover 127.5%
  - **Value**: Total Return 8.6%, Active Return +1.7%, Annual Turnover 20.3% (lowest turnover)
  - **Min Volatility**: Total Return 8.5%, Active Return +1.4%, Risk **11.6%** (lowest risk)
  - **Dividend Yield**: Total Return 8.2%, Active Return +1.2%
  - **Small Cap (Low Size)**: Total Return 7.7%, Active Return +0.9%
- **Factor theories (Exhibit 5)**:
  - Value: risk (systematic) + behavioral (errors-in-expectations, loss aversion) + flows
  - Low Size: risk (higher systematic, liquidity) + behavioral (neglect)
  - Momentum: risk (higher tail risk) + behavioral (under/overreaction) + flows
  - Low Volatility: **NO risk theory** (only behavioral: lottery effect, overconfidence, leverage aversion)
  - Dividend Yield: risk (bird-in-hand) + behavioral (preference for dividends)
  - Quality: **NO risk theory** (only behavioral: errors-in-expectations)
- **Factors that do NOT generate persistent premia**: Growth, Liquidity
- **Cyclicality warning**: All 6 factors have underperformed benchmark for 2-3+ years at minimum
  - Size factor: 6-year underperformance in 1990s (severe cycle)
  - Quality/Momentum: shorter cycles but still significant
- Factor correlations: Low Vol and Quality are most positively correlated; Momentum and Value are most negatively correlated
- Key criterion for persistence: **strong theoretical foundation** is required (purely empirical factors are dangerous)

## Factors That SURVIVE Post-Publication
- **Quality** (highest total return 10.9%): ROE, earnings stability, financial leverage
- **Momentum** (second highest return 10.4%): 12-1 month price momentum
- **Value** (B/M, E/P, CF/P): stable over long periods, lowest turnover
- **Low Volatility / Min Volatility**: unique — NO risk theory, yet most persistent (behavioral-only factor that works)
- **Dividend Yield**: moderate but persistent (risk-based + behavioral)
- **Low Size**: long-run premium but very long cycles

## Factors That DECAY Post-Publication
- **Growth** (P/B growth, earnings growth): does NOT outperform over full cycle
- **Liquidity** (turnover-based): does NOT outperform over full cycle
- Both Growth and Liquidity are identified as "traps" — intuitively appealing but empirically weak

## Korea/International Applicability
- MSCI paper covers DEVELOPED markets (MSCI World), not emerging markets
- Korea is MSCI Emerging Market (partially developed) — factor premia likely similar but potentially larger
- Low Volatility in Korea: particularly relevant given KOSDAQ volatility; behavioral drivers are universal
- Quality factor (10.9% total return): most applicable given Korean accounting data (GPA, ROE from DART)
- Momentum in Korea: historical evidence suggests 6-12 month momentum works but shorter cycles
- Size premium in Korea: large premium historically in Korean small-caps, consistent with global evidence
- Dividend Yield: Korean dividend yield strategies have historically performed well (consistent with MSCI evidence)
- Growth/Liquidity non-performance: important warning — avoid building growth-tilted strategies

## Actionable Implications for Our Pipeline
1. **Quality + Low Volatility** is the best combination by MSCI evidence: Quality gives highest return, Low Vol gives lowest risk. Our STR_011 (Macro-Gated LowVol) already captures Low Vol leg.
2. **Add Quality leg** to LowVol strategy: Quality composite (ROE + GPA + low leverage) combined with Low Vol should deliver Quality's 10.9% with Low Vol's 11.6% risk profile
3. **Value + Momentum combination** is the classic diversifier: negatively correlated factors smooth cycle timing
4. **Avoid Growth factor**: earnings growth, revenue growth NOT persistent premia — do not build growth momentum strategy
5. **Avoid Liquidity factor**: turnover-based signals are NOT persistent premia — remove from factor candidates
6. For factor cycling: MSCI evidence confirms 2-3 year drawdown windows are NORMAL — macro regime gating (as in STR_011) helps identify which factor is in favorable regime
7. **Factor rotation** is viable: Low Vol in high-risk regimes, Momentum in bull regimes, Value in recovery regimes, Quality across all regimes
