# P069: The Impact of Crowding in Alternative Risk Premia Investing (Baltas 2019)
**Category:** Factor_Return / Crowding
**Feasibility:** high (CoMetric is fully computable from price data alone)
**Novelty:** high (extends crowding framework to multi-asset ARP; divergence vs. convergence distinction is highly actionable)

## Core Claim
The impact of crowding differs fundamentally between "divergence premia" (momentum-type, positive-feedback) and "convergence premia" (value-type, negative-feedback). Crowded divergence premia underperform over the subsequent 6–12 months; crowded convergence premia OUTPERFORM. The CoMetric (pairwise excess correlation of factor basket residuals) provides a reliable, price-data-only crowding indicator.

## Crowding Detection Method (exact metrics if available)

**CoMetric (price-based crowding indicator):**
For strategy with N assets, divided into top-scoring (+1) and bottom-scoring (-1) baskets:
1. For each asset i in the top/bottom basket, compute factor-model residual returns over rolling Δ weeks:
   ```
   r_{i,t} = α_i + Σ_j β_{i,j} × F_{j,t} + ε_{i,t}
   ```
   where factor model excludes the factor of interest (e.g., for momentum: market + size + value model)
2. Compute signed average pairwise correlation of residuals:
   ```
   CoMetric_t = [Σ_{i<j} s_{i,t} × s_{j,t} × ρ(ε_{i,t}, ε_{j,t})] / [N × (2N-1)]
   ```
   where s_{i,t} = +1 if asset in top basket, -1 if in bottom basket
3. Rolling Δ = 52 weeks for momentum; 5 × 52 weeks (5 years) for value
4. **Most crowded weeks** = top 20% of CoMetric values; **Least crowded** = bottom 20%

**Data used (multi-asset):**
- Equities: Worldscope/Axioma global developed market universe, Sep 2004–May 2018
- Commodities: 24 S&P GSCI commodities, Bloomberg, Jan 1999–May 2018
- FX: 26 developed/EM currencies, Bloomberg, Jan 2000–May 2018

**Factor model per asset class:**
- Equities: 4-factor (MSCI World + size + value + momentum)
- Commodities: 2-factor (GSCI + carry)
- FX: 3-factor (USD index + carry + momentum)

**Key empirical results (Table 2 — Divergence premia):**
- Equity momentum: High CoMetric → Year 1 return = -5.91%**, Low CoMetric → Year 1 return = +6.86%**; H-L spread = -12.78%**
- Equity low-beta: High CoMetric → Year 1 = -20.11%**, Low CoMetric → Year 1 = +8.74%**; H-L = -28.85%**
- Equity quality: High CoMetric → Year 1 = -4.74%**, H-L = -13.47%**
- FX momentum: High CoMetric → Year 1 = -1.05%, H-L = -2.44%**
- Commodity momentum: H-L = -5.53%**

**Key empirical results (Table 3 — Convergence premia):**
- Equity value: High CoMetric → Year 1 = +5.45%**, Low CoMetric → Year 1 = -11.61%**; H-L = +17.05%**
- FX value: H-L at Year 1 = +3.72%**
- Equity size: inconclusive (size may not be a pure convergence premium)

## Key Results
- Crowding has OPPOSITE effects on divergence vs. convergence strategies
- Divergence (momentum, low-beta, quality): crowded → underperform; uncrowded → outperform
- Convergence (value, FX value): crowded → outperform (crowd accelerates convergence); uncrowded → underperform
- Low-beta (BAB) classified as divergence premium — crowded low-beta periods are particularly risky
- CoMetric is persistent (weeks to months), enabling monthly monitoring
- Volatility targeting partially compensates for momentum crowding risk (divergence premia show higher vol after crowded periods)
- Volatility targeting does NOT help convergence premia (opposite dynamic)
