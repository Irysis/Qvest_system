# P004: Appendix D — The Organization of a QEPM Department (2022)

## Core Finding
A QEPM research department operates most effectively under a "research control" model (vs. "portfolio manager control"), with a five-stage research process: (1) idea generation, (2) data gathering, (3) model building, (4) backtesting/verification, (5) reporting. Incentive structures that link researcher compensation to live portfolio performance — not just paper portfolio backtests — produce superior research output.
