# P121: Trading Volume and Stock Investments
**Authors:** Brown, Crocker, Foerster (2009, FAJ)
**Category:** Factor_Return / Liquidity_Factors

## Core Idea
High-volume stocks outperform low-volume stocks. 12M holding D10-D1 = +6.73% (t=2.87). Effect driven by attention/information, not pure liquidity. Survives FF3+UMD controls.

## Key Results
| Holding | D10-D1 Spread | t-stat |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| 1M | 0.72% | 2.15 |
| 3M | 1.18% | 1.98 |
| 6M | 2.27% | 2.31 |
| 12M | 6.73% | 2.87 |

- Stronger in bull markets, weaker/reverses in bear markets
- TV factor alpha significant after FF3+UMD

## Parameters
- Dvol3mo = mean(Close × Vol) over 63 trading days
- Turnover = (Vol / SharesOut) × 252
- Decile sort, monthly/quarterly rebalance

## Implementation for Korea
- OHLCVS only needed (Close, Vol, Size)
- SharesOut ≈ Size / Close
- CRITICAL: Residual volume (remove market volume) to filter retail noise
- Regime conditioning essential: bull = full signal, bear = reduce/reverse
- Contrasts with Amihud ILLIQ (P100) — test both directions

## Strategy Design → STR_006
- Score = Z(ResidDvol3mo), sector-neutral
- Quality gate: min 200 obs, exclude vol spikes > 5σ
- Regime: bull = long high-vol, bear = neutral or reverse
