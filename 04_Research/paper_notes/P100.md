# P100: Illiquidity and Stock Returns
**Author:** Yakov Amihud (2002, JFM)
**Category:** Factor_Return / Liquidity_Factors

## Core Idea
ILLIQ = avg(|Return| / Dollar Volume) — measures price impact per unit of trading. Higher ILLIQ = more illiquid = higher expected returns (illiquidity premium).

## Key Results
- ILLIQMA coefficient = 0.162 (t = 6.55) in Fama-MacBeth regressions
- Effect strongest for small-cap stocks
- Market-level AILLIQ predicts future market excess returns
- Unexpected illiquidity shocks lower contemporaneous returns
- 10,000+ citations, foundation of liquidity factor models

## Parameters
- ILLIQ_iy = (1/D) * SUM(|R_id| / VOLD_id) over trailing year
- ILLIQMA = ILLIQ / cross-sectional mean (mean-adjusted)
- Dollar Volume = Close × Vol (share volume)
- Min 200 trading days required
- Exclude extreme outliers (top/bottom 1%)

## Implementation for Korea
- Fully implementable with OHLCVS (Close, Ret, Vol, Size)
- DollarVol = Close * Vol in KRW
- KOSPI200+KOSDAQ150 already filters out most illiquid micro-caps
- Effect likely more pronounced in KOSDAQ150 component
- Caution: illiquid stocks have higher trading costs
