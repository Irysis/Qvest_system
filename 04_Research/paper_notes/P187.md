# P187: Trended Momentum
**Authors:** Cai, Li, Keasey (2024, SSRN 4740445)
**Category:** Factor_Investment / Momentum

## Core Idea
Not all momentum is equal — two stocks with identical 12M returns but different price *paths* have different momentum signal quality. **Trend Clarity (TC)** = R² of regressing daily Close on day index. High TC = smooth linear price trajectory → stronger momentum effect.

## Key Results
- Unconditional momentum: 6.90% ann. US (1926-2023)
- High TC quintile momentum: **12.68% ann.** (84% higher)
- Low TC quintile: ~0% momentum (insignificant)
- Survives FF5, Q-factor, behavioral factor models
- **No post-publication decay** (unlike standard momentum)
- **Crash mitigation**: high TC momentum has significantly smaller crash losses

## Parameters
- Formation: t-11 to t-1 (skip month t)
- TC: R² of Price_d ~ d for daily Close in formation window
- Min 200 daily obs required
- Double sort: PRET quintiles × TC quintiles (dependent)
- Holding: 6 months
- **Emerging markets: use terciles, not quintiles**

## Implementation for Korea
- Fully implementable with OHLCVS (Close, Ret, Vol, Size, Sector)
- Use terciles (3×3) for KOSPI200+KOSDAQ150 (~350 stocks)
- Volume interaction: high vol + high TC = strongest momentum
- Skip-month convention: standard short-term reversal avoidance
- Regress actual **price level** (not returns) on date sequence

## Strategy Design → STR_004
- Score = PRET_rank × TC interaction (long: top PRET × top TC)
- Quality gate: min 200 obs, exclude ret < -50%
- Sector neutralize
- Quarterly rebal to control turnover

## Post-Mortem (STR_004: FAIL 19.9/100, CAGR 5.5%, MDD 78.1%)
- **한국 시장 특이성**: TC 고득점 = 주가조작/작전주 (SG사태 라덕연 종목 패턴)
- 수개월간 직선적 상승(R²↑) → 급락(MDD↑) = 전형적 작전주 lifecycle
- US 시장(정보 효율적)과 달리 한국에서는 high-TC가 **역신호**
- **교훈**: 한국에서 TC는 단독 알파 팩터가 아닌 **작전주 탐지 음의 스크리닝 신호**로 활용
- 향후 ensemble에서 TC > 0.9 종목을 **제외 필터**로 사용 검토
- 벤치마크조차 하회(CAGR 5.5% vs BM 9.2%) — 한국 모멘텀은 reversal-dominant 시장
