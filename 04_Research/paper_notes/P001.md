# P001: Appendix A — A Brief History of Financial Theory (2022)

## Core Finding
From Bachelier (1900) through Markowitz, CAPM, Black-Scholes, APT, and Fama-French, 100+ years of financial theory establish the foundations of quantitative equity portfolio management. The multifactor model — extending CAPM to multiple systematic risk factors — is the core framework for QEPM alpha generation.
