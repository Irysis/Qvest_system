## Core Thesis

This is the original Meritz MVAA (Meritz Vigilant Asset Allocation) strategy paper. MVAA adapts Keller's VAA framework to a Korean investor's perspective, using 16 global ETF asset classes with a vigilant (fast-switching) momentum-based protection mechanism. The core insight is that the 20-year US Treasury (미국채20) serves as the baseline safe asset, and the system switches aggressively to it when fewer than 6 of 16 assets have positive momentum.

## Strategy Description

**Universe (16 assets):**
- Equities (8): 코스피(KOSPI), S&P500, 전세계미국제외(ACWI ex US), 선진국미국제외(EAFE), EM, 나스닥100, 러셀2000, 미국하이일드채권(리츠처럼 취급)
- Bonds (3): 미국채20(TLT), 미국채10(IEF), 신흥국채권(EMB)
- Alternatives (3): 금(GLD), 부동산(IYR), 코스피(also serves dual role)
- Benchmark safe asset: 미국채20 (20-year US Treasury)

**Momentum Score:**
- Calculated monthly using 1,3,6,12 month returns with weights 12:4:2:1 (same as VAA 13612W)
- Formula: Score = 12*(p0/p1) + 4*(p0/p3) + 2*(p0/p6) + 1*(p0/p12) - 19
- Equivalent to weighted sum of annualized returns over 4 horizons

**Protection Mechanism (MVAA key rule):**
- Count number of assets with momentum score > 0, call this b (breadth)
- Safe asset weight = (number of assets with momentum <= 0) / 10
  - Example: if 8 of 16 assets have negative momentum → safe asset weight = 8/10 = 80%
- If 6 or fewer assets have positive momentum → safe asset weight = 100% (full defensive)
- Risk asset portion: invest equal weight in Top 4 momentum assets from the 16

**Safe Asset Selection:**
- Default safe asset: 미국채20 (TLT equivalent)
- (Korean implementation note: use Korean bond ETF TIGER 미국채10년선물 or KODEX 미국채울트라30년선물(H))

**Rebalancing:** Monthly, end of month

**Simulation Results (1993.2 - 2020.5):**
- MVAA: CAGR ~17-18%, S&P500 benchmark: CAGR ~10%
- MVAA Max DD: ~-15% vs S&P500 Max DD: ~-50%
- 2020 YTD (through Nov): MVAA +17%, S&P500 +16%
- Key advantage: Reduced stock weight to 0% in Feb-Jun 2020 (COVID crash avoidance)

## Key Results

- MVAA outperforms S&P500 substantially since 1993 with much lower drawdown
- The 6-asset breadth threshold creates fast-switch protection: when markets broadly decline, exits to bonds immediately
- Korean asset (KOSPI) is included in universe → provides Korea-specific allocation signal
- 2020 COVID performance: switched to 100% bonds in February, re-entered gradually from July
- Historical average equity weight: ~32%, bond weight: ~61%, alternatives: ~7%
- Sharpe Ratio significantly higher than buy-and-hold equity
- Strategy is "vigilant" — reacts quickly to broad market deterioration
