# P155: Capacity of Smart Beta Strategies (2018)
**Category:** Smart_Beta | **Priority:** 3

## Core Finding
Smart beta strategies face meaningful capacity constraints: small-cap and equal-weight strategies start degrading at $1–5B AUM, while large-cap factor strategies (value, low-vol) can absorb $10–50B before significant alpha erosion. The key mechanism is price impact at rebalancing — strategies that trade concentrated factor buckets suffer amplified market impact relative to cap-weighted passive.

## Key Risk/Concern for Korean Implementation
KOSPI200+KOSDAQ150 total market cap is approximately $300B USD with average daily turnover far below US levels. Korean market impact costs are 2–3x higher than US equivalents at comparable AUM. Any strategy targeting small-cap or mid-cap factor exposure in Korea faces binding capacity limits well below $500M, making scalability a first-order design constraint from day one.

## Actionable Takeaway
1. Build explicit capacity checks into hurdle_gate.R: compute max_capacity = sum(ADV * 0.05) across portfolio and flag if strategy AUM target exceeds 20% of that figure.
2. Prefer composite factor scores that spread trades across more names over concentrated pure-factor sorts; use score * market_cap weighting (not pure rank) to reduce per-stock trading pressure.
