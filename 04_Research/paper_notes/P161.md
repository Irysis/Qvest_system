# P161: Smart Beta 2016 Global Survey — FTSE Russell (2016)
**Category:** Smart_Beta | **Priority:** 2

## Core Finding
72% of asset owners globally are evaluating smart beta strategies and 36% already have allocations. Primary adoption motivations: return enhancement (69%), risk reduction (52%), cost reduction (44%). Top concerns: factor timing difficulty (48%), lack of long track record (41%), implementation complexity (38%). Multi-factor strategies are gaining most interest (42% of evaluators). The survey signals institutional acceptance of smart beta as mainstream, implying rising AUM and therefore rising crowding risk across all major factors.

## Key Risk/Concern for Korean Implementation
Korean institutional adoption is lagging global peers by approximately 3–5 years but accelerating. The NPS (National Pension Service, ~$800B AUM) and financial holding company pension funds began systematic smart beta allocations post-2018. This creates a delayed crowding wave specifically in the Korean market: factors that have been crowded and compressed globally since 2016 may still appear "fresh" in Korea but will face rapid compression as institutional adoption accelerates.

## Actionable Takeaway
1. Monitor Korean smart beta ETF AUM growth quarterly as a crowding proxy: flag any single factor where ETF AUM > 0.5% of total KOSPI200 float as potentially crowded.
2. Prefer factors with low institutional adoption in Korea (currently: quality/profitability composite, low-accrual) over highly adopted factors (low-vol, dividend yield) which already face Korean crowding pressure.
